About Suhail Sidheeq

Executive Coordination · Business Operations · Human Capital Systems

Suhail Sidheeq Thandu Parakkal is a business professional whose career bridges executive office management, organizational operations, and human resource systems. Grounded in an academic background in Psychology and Human Resource Management, he combines a structured understanding of human behavior with operational discipline and stakeholder coordination.

His professional journey spans healthcare operations, talent acquisition, and corporate environments. At Meitra Hospital and Aster MIMS, he gained intensive experience across end-to-end recruitment, international patient coordination, and hospital administrative operations. His subsequent advisory work at YFYD focused on HR restructuring, standard operating procedure (SOP) development, and optimizing institutional hiring pipelines.

Currently, Suhail serves as Executive Assistant to the Associate Vice President (AVP) Office at MS GES / MS Holdings. In this capacity, he manages executive priorities, coordinates cross-border communications, organizes high-level documentation and briefs, and acts as a dependable coordination bridge between executive leadership and functional teams across regional networks.

Known for discretion, analytical clarity, and proactive execution, Suhail operates with a philosophy centered on continuous learning—actively unlearning and relearning to bring agile, result-oriented value to leadership and evolving business ecosystems.
